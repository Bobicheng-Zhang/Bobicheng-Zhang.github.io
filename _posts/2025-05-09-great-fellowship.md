I will be a graduate student fellow for the Graduate Educator Advancement and Teaching (GREAT) Office, working on a 
series of events to promote more active conversations about disabilities among graduate students. The series will be 
called Graduate Resources for Accommodations and Supportive Practices (GRASP).

Here are some details for the upcoming events:
- [Disability Awareness Lunch and Learn](https://grad.msu.edu/events/grasp-disability-awareness-lunch-and-learn)
- [Workshop: Requesting Accommodations as Graduate Students](https://grad.msu.edu/events/grasp-workshop-requesting-accommodations-graduate-student)
- [Panel: Creating a Culture of Care](https://grad.msu.edu/events/grasp-panel)
